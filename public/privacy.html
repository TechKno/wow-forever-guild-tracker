Your data in the guild ledger

This is an unofficial guild site for Example Guild, EU Roleplay, operated using the guild’s Battle.net API client.

Battle.net sign-in

You sign in on Blizzard’s own website. Your password never reaches this site. With your permission, Blizzard returns your identity and a list of WoW characters so we can verify which characters belong to you. We save a hashed account identifier and keep full character details only for guild characters verified by this guild’s returned EU Forever roster or by an account-owned individual profile confirming the exact guild and EU Roleplay ruleset. During sign-in or an automatic account check, we may check up to eight other account-owned profiles to find guild characters; unrelated names and profile details are not retained. Profile-based membership proof expires after one hour unless a new successful check renews it, and still requires recent Battle.net ownership verification. We do not save your email address.

For automatic character discovery, we keep a private index of character IDs returned by your authorised Battle.net account check, including characters not yet in the guild. This lets the next guild roster check match a known character to its verified account. New guild characters without a matching owner can trigger authorised account checks; accounts are not polled on a regular timer when there are no unmatched arrivals. The ID index is kept for up to 24 hours and deleted on sign-out or expiry. It is never published. Unrelated character names and details are not retained.

We temporarily keep your Battle.net access token encrypted on the server, used only to verify your Battle.net account, recheck character ownership and discover verified guild characters. We keep it until the provider’s expiry or 24 hours after sign-in, whichever comes first, and delete it when you sign out. We do not store refresh tokens or your password. When this connection expires, sign in again to enable further automatic discovery. Levels, professions and recipes use the guild’s separate scheduled refresh.

The site uses secure, HTTP-only cookies for the sign-in flow and a session lasting up to 24 hours. Signing out removes that session. The cookies are needed for sign-in and are not advertising cookies.

Your roster sorting and main/alt grouping choices are saved in this browser, separately for each signed-in account. Only display settings and an account-specific storage key are saved for this purpose. Clearing this site’s browser data removes those choices.

Public and guild-only information

The public welcome page shows a guild introduction and the total number of verified guild characters, including mains and alts, with separate counts for the published roster and independently profile-verified additions. Character names, levels, talent builds, professions, learned recipes, observed progress, main/alt associations, member-selected preferred and primary combat roles, biographies, crafting requests, recipe wishlists and events on the website require Battle.net sign-in and a verified current guild character. Connection settings and administrator mappings have an additional administrator restriction. Member features use the characters you select, rather than your BattleTag.

Guild gallery

Gallery screenshots, titles, captions and uploader display names are visible only to verified guild members. Images are resized before sharing and embedded camera and location metadata are removed. Upload only images you are happy to share with the guild. You can remove or restore your own uploads; the administrator can moderate all uploads. Removed images are hidden and retained for restoration until the site operator removes the stored record. Gallery images are not posted to Discord automatically.

Website activity

The administrator can see Battle.net IDs and BattleTags, connected or mapped Discord IDs and display names, and website sign-in times. The site retains the latest sign-in with each website account and keeps recent sign-in history and daily traffic totals for 90 days. Traffic counters store only daily totals.

History and retention

Character observations are kept for 90 days and milestone history for one year. Expired sign-in attempts and sessions are removed automatically. Guild hall contributions remain until removed by the site operator; cancelled requests and events remain marked cancelled. To request removal of your site account or contributions, contact the guild’s site operator.

Limitations

Blizzard’s Forever account and character APIs may be delayed or incomplete. A missing profile or recipe list does not establish that a character lacks that skill. We retain Blizzard’s returned activity timestamp as a refresh hint. It is not verified online presence or a login/logout history. We do not read private in-game messages.

Discord and quest comparisons

Discord linking is optional. We store your Discord user ID privately and your explicit DM choices. Bot tokens and client secrets stay in server settings and are not returned to your browser; Discord access tokens are discarded after linking. Finished delivery jobs are removed after 30 days. Opt out or unlink under Members & roleplay. Crafting DMs go only to selected, enabled recipients. A personal opt-out remains effective after unlinking. Administrators may assign Discord members to current guild characters for bot notifications and party roles; these assignments do not grant website membership or provider-verified ownership. Dungeon details are shared with the configured guild Discord server. Craft requests without a selected crafter, or with an unavailable selected-crafter DM, can also be posted in the configured crafting channel, including the recipient character name and material requirements. These Discord posts are visible to people who can access that channel. A requester or a linked guild crafter with verified recipe knowledge may confirm completion using a reaction; manual item requests can be confirmed by the requester. Craft posts are queued for deletion on completion or cancellation, while the website retains request history. Discord role reactions synchronise confirmed party signups with the website. Unmatched reactions remain unconfirmed. Reaction identifiers and participant display names may be kept for about 30 days after an event ends. Manual mappings and audit records remain until operator removal. Discord Interested responses are separate from party-role signups.

Quest logs and comparisons are visible only to verified guild members. They are API observations, not live online status or proof of eligibility, location or sharing.
